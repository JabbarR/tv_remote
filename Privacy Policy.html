Privacy Policy for Remote.io

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Thank you for using Remote.io. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

User Provided Information:

We do not collect any personal information during normal use of our app. If you contact us directly for support, you may choose to provide your name, email address, subject, and message.

Usage of Contact Information:

The information you provide through support inquiries is used only to answer your questions, fix issues, and improve our app. We delete this information after resolving your request.

Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party Services:

Our app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect basic device and technical information, such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system version, and ad interaction data. We do not store or process this data directly.

2. Network and Device Permissions

Remote.io operates by connecting to your television via local Wi-Fi or using your device's built-in infrared (IR) hardware. All remote control communication remains strictly local between your smartphone and your TV. We do not log, track, or transmit your TV choices, command history, or local network configuration to external servers.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

Our app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. To learn more about how Google handles this data, please read Google's Privacy Policy.

4. GDPR and Regional Compliance

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, we request your consent before displaying personalized ads. You can modify your ad preferences at any time through the consent options available in the app.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Revised versions will be posted directly to this document.
